🏠 Home Dashboard

A private dashboard that runs on one home PC and shows the owner's own devices and services on their home network.

What it does

It shows the owner's Google Nest thermostat and cameras alongside their other home services, so they can check the temperature, adjust the thermostat and watch their cameras from one page.

Who it's for

Only the owner of this site. It isn't offered to the public, and it's only reachable from inside the owner's home network.

Your data

Nest data stays on the owner's home PC and is never shared. See the privacy policy for details.
